Behind the modelSimple enough to explore. Connected enough to learn. +

This is a simplified educational PWR, not an engineering or operating tool. Four thermal reservoirs exchange energy over time: fuel, primary coolant, secondary water/steam, and the condenser. An ideal generator converts turbine shaft power to electricity; pumps consume a share of that power.

Primary pressure and water inventories are managed automatically. The reactor setting represents a heat-output controller, not a detailed control-rod or neutron model. Steam pressure is estimated from saturated-water temperature. A single decay-heat group preserves residual heat after shutdown. Automatic temperature and pressure trips protect this model; no accident, radiation-release, grid, or demand simulation is included.

Energy is accounted for as electricity, heat rejected, or energy stored. Net efficiency is hidden during strong transients because stored heat can temporarily supply the turbine. Fuel reserve is an illustrative energy budget, not isotope depletion. Time speed affects simulation time, not the physics.
