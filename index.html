Unit 01 · Model information

This is an illustrative PWR operating sandbox, not engineering or operating guidance. Coefficients, limits, actuator travel, damage rates, and emergency equipment are fictionalized for learning.

Operate the equipment

Hold the − / + buttons to change a command. Pump switches retain their speed setting when stopped. Actual pump flow ramps over time; the steam valve travels toward its commanded position. Click a component in the process diagram to locate its controls.

Shutdown does not end cooling

SCRAM suppresses fission. Two effective decay-heat groups and stored energy remain. With primary circulation unavailable, fuel can continue heating after the trip and accumulate irreversible model damage. Auxiliary cooling is an abstract independent heat-removal and makeup system; it is not a detailed real ECCS design. Core damage does not imply an explosion or an off-site release.

Alarms and recovery

ACK ALL marks current alarms as acknowledged. SILENCE stops their audible signal. Neither changes plant conditions. A new or escalated alarm re-annunciates. Damage remains after cooling is restored. Reset experiment clears the simulation, including damage and history; it is not an operating recovery procedure. Low-power operation is not itself an emergency.

Desktop workspace

Designed for 16:9 desktop displays at 1366 × 768 or larger. Customize layout unlocks the side and bottom panel headers. Drag between matching panels, or use their swap buttons. Drag the side-panel edges to resize them. Lock to save the layout in this browser; reset layout restores the default without resetting the plant.

Model boundaries

Four thermal reservoirs, rotor inertia, automatic generator loading, and energy accounting are modeled. Primary pressure is regulated at 155 bar. Inventory loss above the illustrative boiling threshold carries energy away; makeup enters at the fixed 18 °C cooling-sink temperature. Steam pressure is a saturation-temperature estimate, not a complete two-phase model. External auxiliary electricity is assumed available. There is no detailed neutronics, fuel chemistry, grid, containment, radiation-release, or accident-progression calculation.

Sound starts only when enabled. Master volume applies to equipment, clicks, and alarms. Audio is visualized by the same alarm states and is not required to operate the simulator. The simulation pauses while this tab is hidden; sound is suspended too. Faster playback changes simulated time, not integration steps.
